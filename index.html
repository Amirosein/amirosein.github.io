About

I am a Master's student in Astrophysics and Cosmology at the University of Bologna.

My interests lie in observational cosmology and cosmological simulations, with a particular interest in numerical methods, scientific computing, and high-performance computing.

My previous work has involved astronomical observations across optical, radio, and gamma-ray wavelengths, as well as machine-learning methods for astronomical data analysis.

I enjoy combining physics, computational methods, and software development to approach complex scientific problems.

Selected Research

ASTRONOMICAL SPECTROSCOPY

Optical Spectroscopy of Globular Cluster Giant Stars

Reduced and normalized high-resolution optical spectra, measured radial velocities, and derived stellar abundances. Results were validated against reference cluster values using SLAB and SALVADOR.

OBSERVATIONAL ASTRONOMY

Observation Planning for Bootes II RGB Stars

Designed an observing strategy for RGB stars in an ultra-faint dwarf galaxy, optimizing telescope and instrument selection, exposure times, and observing conditions.

RADIO ASTRONOMY

Calibrating and Imaging VLA Data of RBS797

Performed full calibration and imaging of VLA continuum data using CASA. Produced cleaned radio images and measured flux densities and spectral indices.

HIGH-ENERGY ASTROPHYSICS

Fermi-LAT Analysis of TXS 0506+056

Processed five years of gamma-ray data using Fermipy, generating counts maps, light curves, and spectral energy distributions.

MACHINE LEARNING

Machine-Learning Classification of Variable Stars

Developed automated pipelines for light-curve analysis and implemented classification algorithms for variable star types.

HPC · N-BODY SIMULATIONS

Strong Scaling of Gadget-2 Simulations

Ran cosmological N-body simulations on multi-core HPC clusters and evaluated strong-scaling performance and parallel efficiency.
